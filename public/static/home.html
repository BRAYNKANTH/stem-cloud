What is STEM Cloud?

STEM Cloud is a modern, next-generation digitalized learning platform built specifically for Sri Lankan students preparing for their G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) examinations.

It is not just a physics app — STEM Cloud is an all-in-one educational platform engineered to make complex O/L subjects clear, intuitive, and enjoyable. By merging rigorous national curriculum standards with interactive virtual laboratories, character-led story walkthroughs, and smart revision tools, STEM Cloud empowers every learner to succeed.

  • ✓
    Complete G.C.E. O/L Level Lessons: Curated chapters with notes, textbook exercises, worked examples, and glossary.
  • ✓
    Hands-on Digitalized Simulations: Run science and physics experiments right on your smartphone screen.
  • ✓
    Bilingual & Neural Voice Assistance: Tamil and English read-aloud support designed for local curricula.
  • ✓
    Equitable & Offline Accessible: Installable PWA that works offline on low-end phones and weak data connections.

Curriculum Blueprint

G.C.E. Ordinary Level Course Map

Structured alignment with the Sri Lanka National Institute of Education (NIE) syllabus. Master fundamental and advanced modules with guided clarity.

GRADE 10 UNIT 01
Physics is Easy & Fun

Introduction to scientific intuition, measurement fundamentals, and overcoming physics hesitation.

GRADE 10 UNIT 02
Motion in a Straight Line

Displacement, velocity, acceleration, ticker tape timers, and velocity-time graphs.

GRADE 10 UNIT 04
Newton's Laws of Motion

Inertia, F = ma, action and reaction, and practical applications in transport and sports.

GRADE 10 UNIT 05
Friction & Surface Interaction

Static vs limiting vs dynamic friction, coefficients of friction, and lubrication.

GRADE 10 UNIT 09
Resultant Force & Vectors

Parallel forces, vector addition, resolution, and equilibrium of coplanar forces.

GRADE 10 UNIT 11
Turning Effect of Forces

Torque, principle of moments, clockwise vs anticlockwise moments, and levers.

GRADE 10 UNIT 12
Equilibrium & Stability

Stable, unstable, and neutral equilibrium, center of gravity, and base area.

GRADE 10 UNIT 15
Hydrostatic Pressure

Pressure in fluids, liquid column formula P = hρg, transmission, and U-tube manometers.

GRADE 10 UNIT 18
Work, Energy & Power

Kinetic & potential energy, conservation of energy, work done calculations, and power ratings.

GRADE 10 UNIT 19
Current Electricity

Electric potential, Ohm's law (V = IR), series and parallel circuits, and resistor combinations.

GRADE 11 UNIT 04
Waves & Sound

Transverse and longitudinal waves, wave equation v = fλ, frequency, reflection, and resonance.

GRADE 11 UNIT 05
Geometrical Optics

Refraction, Snell's law, convex and concave lenses, ray diagrams, and optical instruments.

GRADE 11 UNIT 09
Heat & Thermal Physics

Specific heat capacity Q = mcΔθ, heat transfer, latent heat, evaporation, and thermal expansion.

GRADE 11 UNIT 10
Domestic Electric Appliances

Household wiring, earthing, fuses, circuit breakers, power consumption, and safety.

GRADE 11 UNIT 11
Electronics & Semiconductors

Diodes, rectification, transistors as switches and amplifiers, and logic gate basics.

GRADE 11 UNIT 13
Electromagnetism

Magnetic field lines, Fleming's left-hand rule, electric motors, induction, and transformers.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about STEM Cloud, accounts, curriculum, and devices.

Is STEM Cloud only for G.C.E. O/L Physics?
No! STEM Cloud is built as an extensible digitalized learning platform for G.C.E. Ordinary Level education. While our physics curriculum features our flagship animated cartoon walkthroughs, interactive virtual laboratories, and worked textbook solutions, the platform is designed for broader STEM subjects, structured lesson delivery, and past paper practice across the curriculum.
How does STEM Cloud work offline?
STEM Cloud is a Progressive Web App (PWA). When you open chapters on your smartphone or computer, the core educational materials are securely cached on your device. You can review notes, reread stories, and study offline without consuming additional mobile data. Your earned XP and stars sync automatically the next time you reconnect!
How do students receive their account credentials?
To ensure personalized progress tracking and prevent account abuse, student accounts are created by our platform administrators or partnered school teachers. You can contact our administrator directly on WhatsApp at 070 775 2433 to enroll and receive your login details.
What languages are supported?
STEM Cloud features full bilingual support for Tamil and English, with high-quality neural voice read-aloud recordings and synchronized text highlighting. Sinhala language localized navigation and curriculum modules are actively in development.
Can I use STEM Cloud on multiple devices?
Yes! You can log in from your smartphone, tablet, or home PC. The platform maintains a secure two-active-device session system to protect your account, and your XP, badges, and completed lessons seamlessly follow you anywhere.
