What we keep

Only what the lessons need: your username, the nickname you chose, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and your learning progress (XP, badges, stars, which lessons you finished, language and theme).

What we don't keep

No email address, phone number, real name, location, or tracking. No adverts. Nothing is shared with other companies.

Who can see it

You, and the site admin (who can see your nickname, username, XP and badge count, and can reset your password or remove your account).

Your choices

You can change your name or password, or delete your account and all saved progress at any time from My account.

Students: please use a nickname and a password you don't use anywhere else.
